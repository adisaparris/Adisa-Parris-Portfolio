Portfolio Six projects · 2023—2025

Selected work

  1. 01

    2024

    Maggio's Wood Fired Pizza

    Brand identity · Print · Illustration

    A neighbourhood pizzeria in Wauwatosa needed a mark that could carry heat. The identity is built around a hand-drawn wood-fired oven — ochre flame, stacked cordwood, hand-set brick — paired with a warm script wordmark.

    The system runs from business cards through a loyalty card to a one-year anniversary poster, held together by a deep teal, coral and cream palette that reads as warm at a distance and detailed up close.

  2. 02

    2024

    Saltwater Condos

    Logo system · Badge lockup

    A home-automation company for coastal properties, drawn as a heritage badge: a gabled house above stylised waves, ringed by a scalloped border and a since-2000 date stamp.

    Built in two weights — a full-ground version for signage and a ridged-edge version for small applications — so the mark holds at every size without redrawing.

  3. 03

    2023

    The Future of Typography

    Poster · Editorial typography

    A lecture-series poster for the UW–Whitewater graphic design program. Outlined display letterforms fracture across a rigid diagonal grid while connective rules thread five speaker biographies through the composition.

    The poster is the argument: typography as structure, not ornament.

  4. 04

    2025

    PetWell

    Logo · Character mark

    A veterinary-care mark built on a peeking corgi — heavy uniform outline, flat colour, a paw raised over the frame edge. Designed to survive at favicon scale and on a clinic wall.

  5. 05

    2023

    Travel Bird

    Logo · Minimal mark

    A penguin with a rolling suitcase, reduced to a single solid silhouette on a horizon line. One weight, one colour, no gradients — a mark that works stamped, embroidered, or foiled.

  6. 06

    Ongoing

    Field Studies

    Illustration · Watercolour

    Ongoing personal work. Sunflowers and lavender in watercolour and coloured pencil — the source of the hand that shows up in every commercial project above.

About

I'm Adisa Parris, a graphic designer working across brand identity, illustration and print.

I draw first. Most of my identities start as a pencil sketch or a watercolour study, and the system gets built around the mark rather than the other way round — which is why the work tends to feel warm even when it's rigorously structured.

Currently open to freelance and full-time work.
